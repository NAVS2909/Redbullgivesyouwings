Red Bull is an energy drink brand founded in 1987. The brand is widely recognised for its distinctive identity and its involvement in sports, racing, adventure, music and entertainment.

This school project presents Red Bull as an advertising campaign, showing how strong branding, visual design and a clear message can create a memorable customer experience.
